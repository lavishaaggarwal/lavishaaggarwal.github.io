Lavisha Aggarwal

Hi! I'm a CV/ML Engineer at Google in the AI Interface team within the Augmented Reality org. I graduated with a Masters in Computer Science from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). Prior to UIUC, I completed my Bachelors in Electrical Engineering from IIT Kanpur, India.

Prior to Google, I was an Applied Scientist at Amazon One, working with Dr. Manoj Aggarwal and Prof. Gerard Medioni

I have been fortunate to intern at Amazon Alexa, Automatic Speech Recognition team in Summer 2018, EPFL Switzerland working with Prof. Elisa Celis in Summer 2016, and MIPS Lab, IIT Kanpur with Prof. Rajesh M. Hegde in Summer 2015.
